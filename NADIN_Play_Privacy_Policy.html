NADIN Play Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 25, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how NADIN Play collects, uses, stores, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Google account information used for sign-in.
  • Email address.
  • Display name and username.
  • Profile picture.
  • Uploaded videos.
  • Comments and other content you create.
  • Watch history.
  • Search history.
  • Saved videos and subscriptions.
  • Device information, IP address, operating system, and app version.
  • Diagnostic logs for troubleshooting and security.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Stream videos.
  • Recommend content.
  • Improve platform performance.
  • Detect spam, fraud, and abuse.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Send important service notifications.

3. Storage

Videos and media may be stored using cloud storage services.

Account information may be stored in secure databases operated by NADIN Play or trusted infrastructure providers.

4. Third-Party Services

NADIN Play may use third-party services including:

  • Google Sign-In
  • Cloud hosting providers
  • Analytics providers
  • Content delivery networks

These providers process data only as needed to deliver their services.

5. Data Security

We implement technical and organizational measures to help protect your information. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method is completely secure. While we strive to safeguard your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Choices

You may:

  • Update your profile information.
  • Delete uploaded videos.
  • Manage notifications.
  • Delete your account, subject to applicable legal or operational retention requirements.

7. Children's Privacy

NADIN Play is not intended for users below the minimum legal age permitted by applicable law without parental or guardian consent.

8. Policy Updates

We may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the Service where appropriate.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the support options available within NADIN Play.
