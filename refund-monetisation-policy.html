Refund & Monetisation Policy

NADIN Play is a digital entertainment platform that allows users to upload, view, interact with and, where applicable, monetize content.

Certain features of NADIN Play may involve payments, earnings, withdrawals, transfers, rewards, purchases, subscriptions or other financial transactions.

By using any payment or monetisation feature on NADIN Play, you agree to this Refund & Monetisation Policy.

NADIN Play is not responsible for delays, failures, declined transactions, technical problems, network failures, banking restrictions, payment-provider failures, reversals or other events caused by third-party financial or payment systems.

Important: A transaction shown as initiated, pending or successful in the application does not necessarily mean that the funds have been finally settled or are immediately available for withdrawal.

3. Transaction Verification

NADIN Play reserves the right, subject to applicable law, to verify, review, delay, suspend, restrict or temporarily hold any payment, earning, withdrawal or transfer where additional verification or investigation is reasonably considered necessary.

This may include situations involving:

  • Suspected fraud or attempted fraud
  • Unauthorized transactions
  • Unusual or suspicious transaction activity
  • Abuse or manipulation of monetisation systems
  • Fake, misleading or manipulated activity
  • Use of bots, automation or prohibited systems
  • Account compromise
  • Duplicate transactions
  • Chargebacks or payment reversals
  • Violation of NADIN Play policies
  • Violation of applicable laws or regulations
  • Disputes regarding ownership or entitlement to funds
  • Other circumstances requiring security or compliance review

Funds may remain temporarily on hold until the relevant issue has been reviewed and resolved.

4. Fraudulent or Suspicious Activity

If NADIN Play identifies activity that appears fraudulent, suspicious, abusive or otherwise prohibited, NADIN Play may, subject to applicable law, suspend or restrict the account, restrict monetisation or withdrawals, place pending earnings on hold, cancel or reverse transactions where permitted, or take other appropriate protective measures.

These measures may apply even where the suspected activity involves only a small number of transactions or a relatively small amount if the activity indicates a potential security, fraud or policy risk.

NADIN Play may request additional information or verification before releasing funds.

5. Earnings and Transfers

Where NADIN Play provides creator earnings or other monetisation features, amounts displayed as earnings may be provisional until the applicable verification, settlement and payment process has been completed.

NADIN Play does not guarantee that every amount displayed as earnings will be transferred immediately or successfully.

Subject to applicable law, amounts may be adjusted, withheld, cancelled or reversed where the underlying transaction is found to be invalid, fraudulent, unauthorized, reversed, disputed or generated through prohibited activity.

6. No Guarantee of Payment Timing

NADIN Play does not guarantee a specific payment or withdrawal processing time unless a specific timeframe is expressly stated for the applicable feature.

Payment timing may depend on:

  • Payment providers
  • Banks and financial institutions
  • Account verification
  • Transaction verification
  • Technical systems
  • Banking schedules and holidays
  • Chargebacks or payment reversals
  • Security checks
  • Legal or regulatory requirements

7. Refund Policy

Refunds are not automatically guaranteed for every transaction.

Where a refund is requested, NADIN Play may review the transaction and determine whether the request qualifies based on the applicable transaction terms, the nature of the service or digital benefit, applicable law and the rules of the relevant payment provider.

Subject to applicable law, a refund may be refused where:

  • The transaction was completed and the applicable service or digital benefit was already provided.
  • The transaction was made in violation of NADIN Play policies.
  • Fraudulent, abusive or manipulative activity is suspected.
  • The transaction has already been refunded or reversed.
  • The refund request does not satisfy the applicable terms.
  • The payment provider or applicable law does not permit the requested refund.

Nothing in this policy is intended to exclude or restrict any refund, cancellation or consumer right that cannot legally be excluded or restricted.

Where NADIN Play determines that a refund is applicable, the refund will normally be processed through the original payment method or another appropriate method supported by the relevant payment provider.

8. Unauthorized Transactions

If you believe that a payment was made without your authorization, you should contact NADIN Play as soon as possible and, where appropriate, contact your bank or payment provider.

NADIN Play may investigate the transaction and may request information reasonably necessary to determine whether the transaction was authorized.

9. Chargebacks and Payment Reversals

If a payment is reversed, charged back or otherwise cancelled after a benefit, reward, service or monetised amount has already been credited, NADIN Play may, subject to applicable law, reverse the corresponding credit or adjust the relevant account balance.

NADIN Play may temporarily restrict related account features while the transaction is being investigated.

10. Account Suspension and Financial Holds

If an account is suspended or restricted because of suspected fraud, abuse, policy violations or legitimate security or compliance concerns, associated monetisation or withdrawal functionality may also be temporarily suspended.

Pending funds may remain subject to verification and applicable legal requirements.

Suspension of an account does not automatically mean that funds are forfeited. The treatment of funds will depend on the circumstances of the transaction, applicable policies and applicable law.

11. Third-Party Payment Providers

Where a payment is processed by a third-party payment provider, that provider's terms and conditions may also apply.

NADIN Play is not responsible for failures caused solely by a third-party payment provider, bank, card network or other external financial infrastructure.

Users should review the terms and policies of the relevant payment provider where applicable.

12. No Guarantee Against Transaction Loss

NADIN Play does not guarantee that every payment, transfer, withdrawal or monetisation transaction will be completed successfully.

NADIN Play will take reasonable measures to operate its payment and monetisation systems securely. However, technical failures, fraud, unauthorized activity, payment-provider issues, banking failures and other circumstances outside NADIN Play's reasonable control may affect transactions.

13. Platform Discretion

To protect users, creators and the platform, NADIN Play reserves the right, subject to applicable law, to modify, suspend, restrict or discontinue payment and monetisation features.

NADIN Play may also introduce additional verification requirements, transaction limits, eligibility requirements or security measures when reasonably necessary.
