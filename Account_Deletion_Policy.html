Account Deletion Policy

Effective Date: August 1, 2026

Welcome to NADIN Play. We respect your privacy and your right to control your personal information. This Account Deletion Policy explains how you can permanently delete your NADIN Play account, what happens to your information after deletion, and how we process your request.

1. How to Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your NADIN Play account at any time by following these steps:

Profile → Edit Profile → Account → Danger Zone → Delete Account

Select "Permanently Remove Your Data" and follow the on-screen confirmation process.

For your security, you may be required to confirm your identity before your deletion request is processed.

2. What Happens When You Delete Your Account

Once your deletion request is confirmed:

  • Your NADIN Play account will be permanently deleted.
  • Your profile information and profile picture will be permanently removed.
  • All uploaded videos and Vynks will be permanently deleted.
  • Your comments, likes, followers, following relationships, notifications, watch history, and saved content will be permanently removed.
  • Media files stored on our cloud storage will be permanently deleted.
  • You will immediately lose access to your account and all associated services.

This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

3. Information That May Be Retained

We may retain limited information where required by applicable law or for legitimate purposes such as:

  • Complying with legal obligations.
  • Preventing fraud, abuse, or security threats.
  • Resolving legal disputes.
  • Enforcing our Terms of Service.

Any retained information will only be kept for as long as necessary and in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

4. Processing Time

Most account deletion requests are processed within 30 days of confirmation. Certain backup systems or legally required records may require additional time before they are permanently removed.

5. Account Recovery

After your account has been permanently deleted:

  • Your account cannot be restored.
  • Your data cannot be recovered.
  • If you wish to use NADIN Play again, you must create a new account.

6. Third-Party Sign-In

If you signed in using a third-party authentication provider (such as Google), deleting your NADIN Play account does not automatically delete your account with that provider. You must manage your third-party account separately through the respective provider.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding account deletion or your personal data, please contact the NADIN Play Support Team through the contact information provided within the application or on our official website.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Account Deletion Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, legal requirements, or privacy practices. The latest version will always be available under About NADIN Play → Legal → Account Deletion Policy.
